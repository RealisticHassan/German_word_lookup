Privacy Policy – German Word Lookup

German Word Lookup does not collect, store, or share personal user data.

When a user highlights a word on a webpage, the selected text is sent to Wiktionary (https://wiktionary.org) to retrieve definitions and conjugation information. This data is used only to provide the extension’s functionality and is not stored or shared elsewhere.

The extension uses Chrome storage to save words locally in the user’s browser. These saved words are not transmitted to any external servers.

The extension does not:
- Collect personal information
- Track browsing history
- Sell or share user data
- Use analytics or tracking tools

Users can delete all saved data at any time through the extension popup.

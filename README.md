# 🇩🇪 German Word Lookup

### Read German online — and actually remember what you learn.

You're reading a German article, a news page, or a study text. You come across a word you don't know. Normally you'd open a new tab, search it, try to remember it, and forget it by tomorrow.

**This extension changes that.** It turns every German webpage into an interactive lesson — look up any word in one click, save it, and quiz yourself later. All without ever leaving the page you're reading.

## 🔁 Your Daily Learning Loop

Most people look up a word and forget it the next day. This extension is designed around a simple loop that makes sure that doesn't happen:

---

### Step 1 — Highlight any German word

While reading any webpage, simply select a German word with your mouse. A small **"German ?"** badge will appear near your selection.

> You don't need to open a dictionary, copy the word, or switch tabs. Everything happens right where you are.

---

### Step 2 — See the full lookup instantly

Click the badge and a tooltip pops up with everything you need to understand the word:

- 📖 **English definition** — clear, plain-English meaning
- ⚧ **Grammatical gender** — color-coded badge: **der** (blue) · **die** (red) · **das** (green)
- 📚 **Plural form** — shown right next to the gender for every noun
- 🏷️ **Part of speech** — Verb, Noun, Adjective, Adverb, etc.
- 🔑 **Verb conjugation table** — for verbs, see all four tenses: Präsens, Präteritum, Partizip II, and Imperativ
- 🧠 **Smart base-form detection** — if you highlight *bietet*, it automatically looks up *bieten* (the base verb)
- 🌐 **Phrase translation** — highlight a full phrase instead of one word to get a sentence-level English translation
- 🔊 **Pronunciation** — click the speaker icon to hear the word spoken aloud in German

> **Pro tip:** Click the 📌 pin icon to lock the tooltip in place so it stays visible while you continue reading.

---

### Step 3 — Save the word to your personal list

Click **"Save Word"** in the tooltip. The word is added to your vocabulary list along with:

- Its gender, plural, and definition
- The exact sentence you found it in — so you always remember the context
- The date you saved it

Your list is organized **day by day**. Words saved today appear under "Today", yesterday's under "Yesterday", and so on. Click any day to expand or collapse it, keeping your list clean and easy to browse.

> Your words are saved privately in your browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

---

### Step 4 — Quiz yourself with flashcards

Open the extension by clicking its icon in your Chrome toolbar. Hit the **🧠 Quiz** button.

Your saved words become a set of flashcards. For each card:

1. You see the **German word**
2. You try to recall what it means
3. Click **"Reveal Answer"** to see the definition, gender, and the sentence you found it in
4. Rate how well you remembered it: **Again · Hard · Good · Easy**

Based on your rating, the extension automatically decides when to show you that word again:

- Rate it **Easy** → it won't appear again for a while
- Rate it **Hard** → it comes back in a day or two
- Rate it **Again** → it comes back very soon

This is called **spaced repetition** — the same method used by Anki and professional language learning apps. The words you struggle with come back more often. The ones you know well fade into the background. Over time, your weak words become strong ones.

> The Quiz button always shows how many cards are due — for example **"🧠 Quiz · 5 due"** — so you know at a glance whether it's worth opening.

---

### Step 5 — Retry only the hard words

Already done a full quiz session? Enable **"⚡ Retry Hard & Again cards"** toggle in the popup.

This switches the quiz into focus mode:

- Only words you previously rated **Hard** or **Again** will appear
- Words you've already mastered are skipped entirely
- The button shows exactly how many cards need attention — e.g. **"🧠 Quiz · 3 to retry"**
- If you've mastered everything, it tells you: **"🧠 All mastered ✓"**
- If you haven't taken a quiz yet, it guides you: **"🧠 Quiz first"**

> This is perfect for a quick 5-minute review session where you want to focus on what actually needs work — not repeat words you already know.

## 📊 Track Your Progress

### 🔥 Daily Streak
Save at least one word every day to build your streak. Your current streak is shown at the top of the popup. Miss a day and it resets — so it gives you a real reason to open the app daily, even if just to save one word.

### 📊 Statistics Screen
Click **📊 Stats** in the popup to see:
- Total words saved
- Your current day streak
- Number of quiz sessions completed
- A 7-day bar chart showing how many words you saved each day

## ⚙️ Extra Features

### 🌐 Better Translations with DeepL *(optional)*
By default, phrase translations use a free service. If you want higher quality, you can connect your own free DeepL account in Settings. Your key is stored only in your browser — we never see it.

### ⬇️ Export Your Word List
Go to **⚙️ Settings → My Words → Export as CSV** to download your full vocabulary list. Open it in Excel, Google Sheets, or import it into Anki for even more study options.

### ⏸ Pause the Extension Anytime
If you're on a page where you don't want the extension active, click the **⏸** button in the popup header to pause it instantly. Click again to resume.

### 🎨 Personalize the Look
- **Dark mode** — toggle the 🌙 button for a comfortable night-time reading experience
- **Three accent colors** — Blue, Teal, or Purple
- **Tooltip size** — Small, Medium, or Large depending on your screen and preference
